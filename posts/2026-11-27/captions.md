# 2026-11-27 — Margin call kya hai

## Instagram carousel
Account mein paisa hai, phir bhi trade apne aap band ho gaya? 😬 Yeh Margin Call hai.

Margin Call simple bhasha mein:
✅ Margin = trade ki value ÷ leverage (0.10 lot EURUSD, 1:100 = $100 margin)
✅ Margin level = equity ÷ margin × 100 ($1,000 ÷ $100 = 1000%)
✅ Loss badhne par level girta hai; bahut kam hua to broker trade band kar sakta hai
✅ Bachne ke liye: 1% risk, hamesha stop loss, poora leverage kabhi use mat karo
$1,000 account par 1 lot = level sirf 100%. (Sirf samajhne ke liye example, signal nahi.)
