Do trade khole aur risk chupke se double ho gaya? Yeh Currency Correlation hai.

Correlation ko samjho:
• Correlation -1 se +1 ke beech hota hai (+1 = same direction, -1 = ulti direction)
• EURUSD aur USDCHF mein dollar common hai, isliye aksar ulti chaal chalte hain
• Example: EURUSD buy + USDCHF sell = dono dollar weak par bet, 1% + 1% asal mein ~2% risk
• Bachne ke liye: trade se pehle pair ka rishta dekho, total risk 1% ke aas-paas rakho, stop loss lagao
(Sirf samajhne ke liye, signal nahi. Purana correlation aage bhi chalega, iski guarantee nahi.)
