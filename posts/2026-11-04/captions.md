Aaj seekho Higher High / Higher Low:
✅ Uptrend: Higher High (HH) + Higher Low (HL), jaise peaks 100, 110, 120 aur dips 95, 103, 112
✅ Downtrend: Lower High (LH) + Lower Low (LL)
✅ Range: price ek zone mein upar-neeche, clear trend nahi
✅ Uptrend mein HL ya downtrend mein LH tootna trend kamzor hone ka hint ho sakta hai
