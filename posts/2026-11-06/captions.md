Aaj seekho Pin bar / Hammer candle:
✅ Hammer: body chhoti (upar), neeche ki wick lambi (body ki 2x+), upar wick na ke barabar
✅ Example: Low 1.0970, Open 1.1000, Close 1.1010, High 1.1012 = body 10 pips, neeche wick 30 pips, upar wick 2 pips
✅ Lambi wick = price ka rejection (sellers ne dhakela, buyers ne wapas khicha)
✅ Context zaroori: support ke paas + agli candle se confirmation. Akeli candle sirf ek clue hai, guarantee nahi.
