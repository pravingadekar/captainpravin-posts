Aaj seekho Moving Average 50/200:
✅ MA = last N candles ke close ka average
✅ Example: closes 1.1000, 1.1010, 1.1020, 1.1030, 1.1040 = total 5.5100, MA(5) = 5.5100 ÷ 5 = 1.1020
✅ 50 MA short-term, 200 MA long-term trend dikhata hai
✅ Golden Cross / Death Cross sirf context hain, signal nahi. MA lagging hai, sideways market mein bar bar cross karta hai.
