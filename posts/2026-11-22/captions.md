# 2026-11-22 — Breakout vs fake breakout

## Instagram carousel
Breakout ke baad market ulta kyun bhaagta hai? 🤔 Har breakout asli nahi hota.

Aaj seekho breakout vs fake breakout:
✅ Asli breakout: candle level ke paar CLOSE hoti hai, body badi, wick chhoti
✅ Fake breakout: wick level ke paar jaati hai par close andar, agli candle wapas andar
✅ Example: EURUSD resistance 1.0900, high 1.0915, close 1.0895 = fake breakout (sirf example)
✅ Risk kam karne ke liye candle close ka wait karo, retest dekho, SL level ke doosri taraf, risk 1%
