# 2026-11-08 — Doji ka matlab

## Instagram (carousel + reel)
Kya buyers aur sellers bilkul barabar ho sakte hain? ⚖️

Aaj seekho Doji candle:
✅ Open aur Close lagbhag same, body bahut chhoti, upar-neeche wick ho sakti hai
✅ Example: Open 1.1000, Close 1.1002 = body 2 pips. High 1.1030, Low 1.0970 = range 60 pips
✅ Matlab: buyers aur sellers ki takkar barabar rahi, market mein pause ya uljhan dikhta hai
✅ Context zaroori: badi move ke baad + confirmation. Akeli doji sirf ek clue hai, guarantee nahi.
