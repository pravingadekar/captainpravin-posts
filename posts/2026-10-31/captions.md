# 2026-10-31 — Risk:Reward 1:2 ka maths (win rate vs RR)

## Instagram (carousel + reel)
Sirf 4 trade jeet kar bhi profit? Jawab Risk:Reward mein chhupa hai. 🤔

Aaj seekho Risk:Reward 1:2:
✅ Risk $10, Reward $20 = RR 1:2
✅ Breakeven win rate = 1 ÷ (1 + RR): RR 1:1 = 50%, 1:2 = ~33%, 1:3 = 25%
✅ 10 trade, 4 jeete, risk $10 har trade: RR 1:2 = +$20, RR 1:1 = -$20
(Sirf example hai, profit ki guarantee nahi. Spread/commission ignore kiye hain.)
