Aaj seekho spread aur commission:
✅ Spread = Ask - Bid. Example: 1.10012 - 1.10000 = 1.2 pips
✅ EURUSD 0.10 lot = $1/pip, to 1.2 pip spread = $1.20 per trade (20 trades = $24, example)
✅ Standard account: spread zyada, commission nahi. Raw/ECN: spread kam, commission alag.
✅ Example: raw spread 0.1 pip = $0.10 + commission $0.70 = $0.80 total
