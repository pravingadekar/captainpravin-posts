## Instagram (carousel + reel)
Demo mein jeette ho, live mein kyun haarte ho? Strategy wahi, badalta hai sirf mann. 🧠

Demo vs Live ka fark:
✅ Demo mein paisa asli nahi, isliye darr nahi lagta
✅ Live mein darr, lalach aur umeed rules todte hain
✅ Live mein chhota risk rakho, jaise 0.5%, aur journal likho

Example (sirf samajhne ke liye): ek hi trade, risk $10, target $20. Demo mein plan poora, live mein darr se $4 par bahar. Plan toota, rules nahi.
