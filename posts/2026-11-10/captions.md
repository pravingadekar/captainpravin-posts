# 2026-11-10 — RSI overbought/oversold ka sahi use

## Instagram (carousel + reel)
RSI 70 par sell? Itna aasan nahi! 📉

Aaj seekho RSI ka sahi use:
✅ RSI momentum indicator hai, scale 0 se 100, default 14 candles
✅ RSI = 100 - 100 ÷ (1 + RS). Example: avg gain 0.6, avg loss 0.4, RS = 1.5, RSI = 100 - 40 = 60
✅ 70 se upar overbought, 30 se neeche oversold. Ye reversal ka signal nahi hai.
✅ Strong trend mein RSI 70 se upar reh sakta hai. Trend aur support/resistance se confirmation lo.
Stop loss aur risk hamesha manage karo. (Sirf educational jaankari hai.)
