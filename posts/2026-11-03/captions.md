# 2026-11-03 — Support aur Resistance kaise draw karein

## Instagram (carousel + reel)
Chart pe lines kahan kheechein? 📈 Price kuch levels pe baar baar ruk jaata hai.

Aaj seekho Support aur Resistance:
✅ Support: woh level jahan price neeche aakar baar baar ruka (price ka floor)
✅ Resistance: woh level jahan price upar jaakar baar baar ruka (price ki chhat)
✅ Draw karne ke liye higher timeframe (H4/Daily) kholo, jahan price 2-3 baar mudha wahan mark karo
✅ Line ki jagah zone banao, wicks ko bhi dekho
Koi level 100% kaam nahi karta, isliye risk hamesha manage karo. (Sirf educational jaankari hai.)
