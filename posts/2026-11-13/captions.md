Loss ke baad turant trade? Ruko! Gusse mein li gayi trade sabse mehngi padti hai 🛑

Aaj seekho revenge trading se kaise bachein:
✅ Revenge trading = loss ke baad badla lene ke liye bina plan ki trade.
✅ Signs: turant nayi trade, stop loss hatana, double lot se recover karna.
✅ Example: $1,000 account par 1% risk = $10. Lot double kiya to 2% risk = $20 loss.
✅ Rules: loss ke baad 15 min break, daily max loss 3%, 2 loss lagatar to trading band, journal mein emotion likho.
