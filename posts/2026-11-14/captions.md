Overtrading ke 4 signs:
✅ Roz plan se zyada trades
✅ Har candle par entry ki jaldi
✅ Loss ke baad turant naya trade
✅ Boredom mein trade lena

Hidden cost: spread 1.5 pip maano, to 2 trades = 3 pip aur 10 trades = 15 pip (example only).
Risk bhi judta hai: $1,000 account, $10 per trade, 10 trades saath mein = $100 (10%) risk.
