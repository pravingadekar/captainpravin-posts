## Instagram (carousel + reel)
Ek hi galti baar-baar kyun hoti hai? Kyunki jo likha nahi jaata, wo yaad nahi rehta. 📓

Trading journal ke fayde:
✅ Apni aadatein saaf dikhti hain
✅ Baar-baar ki galti pakad mein aati hai
✅ Andaze ki jagah asli aankde

Har trade mein likho: pair, date/time, entry, SL, TP, risk %, result, trade ka kaaran aur mood.

Example (sirf samajhne ke liye): 20 trades mein 8 jeete, 12 haare. 12 mein se 7 haar revenge ki wajah se. 7 ÷ 12 = lagbhag 58%. Win rate = 8 ÷ 20 × 100 = 40%.
