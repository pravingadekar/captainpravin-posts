# 2026-10-30 — Stop Loss kahan lagayein (structure-based SL)

## Instagram (carousel + reel)
Stop loss baar baar hit hota hai? Galti market ki nahi, SL ki jagah ki hoti hai. 😬

Aaj seekho structure-based stop loss:
✅ Buy setup mein SL swing low ke neeche, sell mein swing high ke upar
✅ Thoda buffer (jaise 5 pips) rakho
✅ Example: entry 1.0875, SL 1.0850 = 25 pips. $1,000 account, 1% risk = $10, lot = $10 ÷ (25 × $0.10) = 0.04 lot
(Sirf samajhne ke liye example, trade signal nahi.)

Stop loss baar baar hit hota hai? Shayad jagah galat hai.

Structure-based SL: buy setup mein recent swing low ke neeche, sell mein swing high ke upar, thode buffer ke saath.
• Example: entry 1.0875, SL 1.0850 = 25 pips (signal nahi, sirf example)
• $1,000 account, 1% risk = $10
• Lot = Risk $ ÷ (SL pips × pip value) = $10 ÷ (25 × $0.10) = 0.04 lot
