Aaj seekho Body, Wick aur Colour:
✅ Har candle ke 4 points: Open, Close, High, Low
✅ Example: Open 1.1000, Close 1.1040 = body 40 pips
✅ Wick = High/Low tak gayi price jo wapas laut gayi (upper 15 pips, lower 10 pips)
✅ Close > Open = bullish, Close < Open = bearish (colour platform pe depend karta hai)
