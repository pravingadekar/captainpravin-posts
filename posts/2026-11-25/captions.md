# 2026-11-25 — Drawdown recovery maths (50% loss = 100% gain chahiye)

## Instagram carousel
50% loss ke baad 50% profit se account wapas aata hai? Nahi! 😬

$1,000 account mein 50% loss = $500 bache. Ab $500 par 50% kamaye to sirf $750. Wapas $1,000 ke liye chahiye 100%!

✅ Recovery % = Loss ÷ (100 - Loss) × 100
✅ 10% loss = 11.1% chahiye
✅ 20% loss = 25% chahiye
✅ 30% loss = 42.9% chahiye
✅ 50% loss = 100% chahiye
Isliye drawdown chhota rakho: 1% risk, hamesha stop loss, lagatar loss par break.
