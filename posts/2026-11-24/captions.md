# 2026-11-24 — Position sizing jab SL bada ho

## Instagram carousel
Stop loss bada hai to lot size kya rakhein? 🤔

Galti: SL bada, lot wahi purana. Ek trade mein hi account ka 5% ud jata hai. 😬
Sahi tareeka: pehle risk fix karo, phir lot nikalo.

✅ Lot = Risk $ ÷ (SL pips × pip value)
✅ $1,000 account, 1% risk = $10
✅ SL 25 pips = 0.04 lot, SL 50 pips = 0.02 lot, SL 100 pips = 0.01 lot
✅ Teeno mein risk sirf $10. SL double to lot aadha!

(Sirf samajhne ke liye example, signal nahi.) Save kar lo 📌
