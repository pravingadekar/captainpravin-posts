# 2026-11-12 — Major, minor, exotic pairs

## Instagram (carousel + reel)
Sab forex pairs ek jaise nahi hote! Pairs ke 3 type hote hain 👀

Aaj seekho major, minor aur exotic pairs:
✅ Major: USD wale 7 pairs (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY...). Volume zyada, spread kam.
✅ Minor: bina USD ke cross pairs (EURGBP, EURJPY, GBPJPY). Spread thoda zyada.
✅ Exotic: major + chhoti currency (USDTRY, USDZAR, USDMXN). Spread bahut zyada, price tezi se jump kar sakti hai.
✅ Example: 0.10 lot par 1 pip spread = $1, 30 pip spread = $30. Real spread broker par depend karta hai.
