✅ Timeframe = ek candle kitne time ki hai (15 min, 1 hour, 4 hour, daily)
✅ Pehle bada chart (daily/4H) pe trend, phir 1H pe support-resistance, phir 15M pe entry timing
✅ Daily pe uptrend ho to 15M ki chhoti girawat aksar pullback hoti hai (sirf example)
✅ 3 timeframe kaafi hain; bade-chhote mein takraav ho to ruko. SL aur risk rules hamesha lagu
