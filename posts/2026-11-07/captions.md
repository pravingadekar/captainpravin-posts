Kya ek candle doosri candle ko poora nigal sakti hai? 🍽️

Aaj seekho Engulfing pattern:
✅ Pehli candle chhoti, doosri candle ki body badi jo pehli body ko poora dhak le (sirf body dekho, wick nahi)
✅ Example: Candle 1 red Open 1.1020, Close 1.1000 = 20 pips. Candle 2 green Open 1.0995, Close 1.1030 = 35 pips
✅ Matlab: pehle sellers aage the, phir buyers ne control liya, momentum badalta dikh sakta hai
✅ Context zaroori: support ke paas + confirmation. Akeli candle sirf ek clue hai, guarantee nahi.
