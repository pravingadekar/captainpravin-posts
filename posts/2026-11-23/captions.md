Bade move ke baad market kahan tak peeche aata hai? 🤔 Fibonacci is pullback ko naapta hai.

Aaj seekho Fibonacci retracement:
✅ Swing low se swing high tak draw karo, beech mein levels ban jaate hain
✅ Key levels: 38.2%, 50%, 61.8% (ye zones hain, pakki jagah nahi)
✅ Example: EURUSD low 1.0800, high 1.0900 = 100 pip move, 50% level = 1.0850 (sirf example)
✅ Akele bharosa mat karo: support/resistance aur candle confirmation ke saath dekho, SL lagao, risk 1%
