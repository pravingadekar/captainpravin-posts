## What wins (apply to every new reel)
1. **Dark/black background, few big glowing candles, pill tags on the chart** (refs 1, 2, 4 = the top 4 by likes).
2. **Topic:** liquidity, stop-loss hunting, traps, fake breakouts beat everything else. Next best: pattern collections.
3. **Hook in 1 s:** a promise of time ("50 SEC" timer, "60 सेकंड में") or a question ("Breakout या Trap?"). Show the chart moving from frame 1.
4. **Structure:** mistake → why it happens → right way (steps) → one rule → CTA. 45–65 s for explainers.
5. **A "BIGGEST MISTAKE" / TRAP moment** with a stamp, shake and SFX around 20–35 % of the reel.
6. **"X vs Y" comparison** with side-by-side cards at the end (ref 2).
7. **Short 15–20 s visual list** (one pattern every ~2.5 s, glowing art) as save-bait; good for repurposing the weekly video (ref 5).
8. **Comment-keyword CTA:** "कमेंट करो PDF → free Hindi guide" (ref 8) for comments; caption ends with "Save karo 📌".
9. Captions: Hinglish/Hindi, big, 2–3 words, spoken word white, upcoming grey, keywords yellow/green.
10. Always keep: our brand, disclaimer, AI label, no profit promise, no buy/sell signal.
